## What to Consider Before Buying

### Capacity vs. Portability
More capacity means more weight. A 2000Wh unit might sound appealing, but if it weighs 60 pounds, you're not taking it anywhere. For most people, 1000-1500Wh offers the best balance.

### Battery Chemistry
LFP batteries cost more upfront but last 3-5x longer than standard lithium-ion. Over a 10-year period, LFP is actually cheaper per cycle.

### Charging Options
Look for units that support:
- Fast AC charging (under 2 hours to 80%)
- Solar input (400W minimum for practical recharging)
- Car charging (12V for charging during travel)

### Real Output vs. Rated Output
Many units advertise peak surge output but can't sustain it. Look for continuous AC output ratings — that's what actually matters for running appliances.

### Expandability
Some systems allow you to chain multiple units or add extra battery packs. If you might scale up later, check compatibility.

## The Solar Pairing Question

Portable power stations and solar panels are natural partners, but the pairing matters. Most power stations accept 100-500W solar input. For the EcoFlow DELTA 2's 500W max solar input, you'd want 2-3 portable 200W panels.

Realistic solar charging expectations:

- Full sun conditions: 4-6 hours for 80% charge
- Partial clouds: 6-9 hours
- Overcast: 10-12 hours (or minimal charging)

Solar makes sense for extended off-grid trips or as backup to backup power. For emergency home use, the fast AC charging is more practical.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Underbuying capacity**: Calculate your actual power needs. A 500Wh unit sounds sufficient until you realize your coffee maker alone draws 1000W.

**Ignoring cycle life**: Cheap units with 500-cycle batteries need replacement in 1-2 years of regular use. LFP batteries last 8-10 years.

**Forgetting surge requirements**: Your refrigerator might run on 150W but needs 600W to start. Check surge ratings.

**Not testing before emergencies**: Unbox and test your unit immediately. Learn the interface. Discover any issues while you can still return it.
