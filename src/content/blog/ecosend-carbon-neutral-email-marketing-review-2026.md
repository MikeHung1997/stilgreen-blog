## What's Actually Going On With Email and Emissions?

Scope 3 emissions — the indirect emissions in your value chain that don't come from your own facilities or company vehicles — are notoriously hard to measure and harder to reduce. But tools and frameworks are catching up. And one of the most overlooked sources of Scope 3 digital emissions is routine marketing activity: the emails, the automations, the drip sequences, the newsletters.

None of this is catastrophic on its own. But "not catastrophic" is a low bar. Brands that are serious about sustainability are increasingly asking: can we keep the performance *and* clean up the footprint?

EcoSend says yes.
